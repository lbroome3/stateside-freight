About Lewis Broome

Lewis Broome III is a freight agent and entrepreneur based in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. For more than nine years he has worked as an independent agent for Landstar System, Inc., building Stateside International Logistics into an agency that handles hazmat loads, fireworks imports, port drayage and truckload freight across all 50 states, Canada and Mexico.

Lewis runs his agency on tools he builds himself, and that work grew into GPT Marketing, which gives local businesses a professional website, steady social media content and the same kind of AI tools at a small-business price.

He also founded the Surrogate Funding Foundation, a vision that began with his father, Lewis Broome Jr. The foundation reaches young men early with mentoring and money lessons, before the system does.
