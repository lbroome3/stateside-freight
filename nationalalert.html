Meet National Alert.

Who you're trusting

Security from someone who has answered the call.

National Alert was formed in 2005 by Charlie Miles, a former reserve deputy with the Richland County Sheriff's Department. Charlie's background in responding to burglaries, property crimes and violent crimes gives him a unique insight into how to design and install security, surveillance and theft deterrent systems that actually work.

As technology changes, National Alert keeps bringing the latest security innovations to customers, both residential and commercial.

Intrusion Protection

State-of-the-art UL listed high security communication devices, the kind used in banks, casinos and jewelry stores. Easy to operate and designed to reduce false alarms.

Video Surveillance

24/7 coverage that helps protect your property from theft, vandalism and employee issues, with integrated systems that are easy to use.

  • Surveillance cameras & CCTV
  • Thermal imaging
  • Commercial and residential

Access Control

From a jewelry store that rings customers in, to a multi-story building that ties cameras, parking garage access and elevator card readers together.

  • Ring-in door entry
  • Card readers by floor
  • Parking garage access

Fire and smoke detection that works with the rest of your security system, at home or at work.
