Skip it if

Questions

Can it run my refrigerator?

Most household fridges average around 100–200 W once running, so a full charge covers several hours, longer if you keep the door shut. Use the calculator above with your fridge's rating.

Can I use it indoors?

Yes. It's a battery with no engine, so there are no fumes. Never run a gas generator indoors.

How do I recharge it with the power out?

Solar panel or your car. Charge it full from the wall before the storm hits.

Will it run central air or a whole house?

No. It's for essentials. For the whole house you need a standby generator.
