Our standard

Every mentor passes a background check and completes training. No mentor is ever alone with a minor, and a parent or guardian signs consent before a young man joins.

Why a circle works

The model is proven.

Our Crossroads circle follows the same idea as Becoming a Man in Chicago: weekly small-group sessions for young men, led by men who have been there. When the University of Chicago Crime Lab tested it in a randomized trial, the results were clear.

Fewer violent-crime arrests50%
Fewer arrests overall35%
More likely to graduate high school on time19%

Source: University of Chicago Crime Lab, Becoming a Man study (2016). These are Becoming a Man's results, not Surrogate Funding's. We will publish our own numbers as we serve.
