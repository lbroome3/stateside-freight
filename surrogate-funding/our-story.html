The founder's story

Reach him before the system does.

Prevention first · Faith, family, finances, order

I’m a father who was paying what I owed, came up $1,400 short, and watched it become $4,900 plus fees. In that cell, young men called me ‘OG’ and asked me how to live. They didn’t need a lecture. They needed someone to reach them before the system did.

So we mentor first, teach the money lessons I learned the hard way, and keep families together when a small bond would tear them apart.

Lewis Broome IIIFounder & President

The name

Why “Surrogate.”

The vision for Surrogate Funding began with my father, Lewis Broome Jr.

Surrogate means one who stands in another’s place. It comes from the Latin surrogare, “to put in place of another.”

That is the whole work. A mentor stands in where a father, a brother, or an uncle is missing. He does not replace anyone. He shows up. And the fund stands in for the few hundred dollars a family does not have, so a small bond does not cost a man his job, his home, or his children.

Surrogate Funding: standing in, with what it takes.
