How it actually thinks

It optimizes freight around YOU. You tell it once how you run — your lanes, your floor per mile, home time, hazmat or not. From then on it works your plan, not a broker's.

It scans in both directions. Before it offers you anything, it takes where you are and where that load delivers — then searches the board at the delivery for your next load. A load that pays $2.90 into a dead zone loses to $2.60 into a town with three loads waiting that fit you. Every offer shows the reload count at delivery, so you see the chain, not just the leg.

The decision comes to your phone. The load, the money, the miles, the reloads waiting — one tap to claim it. First come, first dispatched.

Your phone works the road with you. The portal shows truck parking around you, diesel around you, truck stops — off your phone's own location, one tap into your maps.

3% of linehaul. That's the whole fee. Fuel surcharge, hazmat pay, detention, every accessorial — 100% yours, untouched. No exclusivity, no minimums, cancel with a text.
