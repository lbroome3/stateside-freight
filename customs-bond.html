Renew or Get a Bond

Which bond do you need?

Continuous bond

One-year, $50k-minimum surety covering all your entries and ISF filings nationwide. The right answer for anyone importing more than a few shipments a year — and the one that needs annual renewal before it lapses.

Single-entry bond

Covers one shipment. Fine for a true one-off — but add the separate ISF bond and fees, and two or three shipments a year already cost more than a continuous bond.

ISF (10+2)

Importer Security Filing must be on file before your container sails. Covered automatically under a continuous bond; a separate bond when you go single-entry.
