Can you ship fireworks from Shanghai to the US?

Yes — 1.4G consumer and 1.3G display fireworks, properly declared, on carriers that accept Class 1. You bring the import authorizations (ATF for 1.3G, CPSC-compliant product for 1.4G); we move the freight legally end to end.

What does a fireworks container cost?

More than general cargo — Class 1 slots are limited and the domestic leg needs hazmat-endorsed drivers with placarded equipment. Use the quote tool for a live lane estimate.

Do you deliver to show sites?

Yes — warehouses, magazines and display sites, with multi-stop routing for tour schedules.

What about my customs bond?

Most importers need a continuous customs bond. If yours needs setting up or renewing, see our customs bond page — we coordinate it with the freight.
