Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026. This site is run by Lewis Broome in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina. It covers lewisbroome.com, the gadget pages under /az, and the Broome CRM at broome-crm.vercel.app, which only Lewis and his team can sign in to.

The short version

  • No account is needed to read anything here, and nothing here sells or rents your information.
  • If you fill in a form (a quote, a load, a gadget demo request), the details you type go to Lewis so he can answer you. That is the only reason they are collected.
  • The gadget pages keep a private usage log so Lewis knows which products people care about. No IP address is stored.
  • Product links on the gadget pages are Amazon affiliate links. Lewis earns a small commission when you buy; it costs you nothing extra.

What you give us on purpose

When you submit a form on this site you may provide your name, company, email address, phone number, a pickup and delivery city, and whatever you write in the message. Lewis uses it to reply, quote, or arrange the freight or demo you asked about, and keeps it in the Broome CRM for that relationship. It is not used for any other purpose and is not shared except with the carriers, brokers, or service providers needed to do the work you requested, or when the law requires it. Ask at any time to see or delete what is held about you (see Contact).

Gadget pages: what is counted, and what is not

The pages under /az (the store, the hurricane kit, the DJI page) send small usage events to the Broome CRM: that a page was opened, which product cards scrolled into view, which Buy button was tapped, an Amazon search term typed into the search box, a demo request, a video play, how far down the page you read, and how long you stayed. If you arrived from one of Lewis's YouTube videos, the link carries a short code so he knows which video sent you.

What is not collected: your IP address is never stored. Your browser keeps a random visitor number in its local storage, made up on your first visit and not derived from anything about you, so repeat visits from the same browser can be counted together; clearing your browser's site data resets it. Lewis may add a private name or note to that visitor record himself, for example after you call or message him. Approximate city and region come from the hosting provider's edge network, and the device type, screen size, language and time zone come from your browser. None of it is sold or shared.

The pages use the browser's local storage to keep that visitor number, remember that you have visited before, and which video sent you. No third-party advertising cookies are set.

The site also uses Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and does not track people across sites.

Amazon Associates disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Lewis Broome earns from qualifying purchases. Links to Amazon on the gadget pages carry an affiliate tag. When you click one, Amazon's own privacy notice applies on Amazon's site. Amazon reports to Lewis only totals (clicks, items ordered, commission), never who bought what.

YouTube and Google API Services

The Broome CRM uses YouTube API Services to read statistics for Lewis's own YouTube channel (views, likes, watch time, subscribers) so he can see how his videos perform. It reads only his channel's data after he signs in with his own Google account, and it does not access, store, or share any viewer's data. By using the CRM's YouTube connection, the signed-in channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The connection can be revoked at any time from Google's security settings; the CRM then holds no YouTube credentials.

Where it lives, and for how long

Form submissions and the gadget-page events are stored in the Broome CRM, hosted on Vercel and Supabase in the United States. Form submissions are kept as long as there is a working relationship or a legal reason to keep them. Gadget-page events are kept to build the trends Lewis looks at; they carry no name, email or IP address unless Lewis adds a note himself.

Children

This site is for adults arranging freight or buying gadgets. It is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects nothing from them.
