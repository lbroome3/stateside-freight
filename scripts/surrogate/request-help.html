Community bail fund

Ask for help with a bond.

If you, or someone you love, is being held on a non-violent charge over a bond you cannot pay, tell us here. A family member, friend, pastor or attorney can fill this out for the person inside. We read every request ourselves.

We post cash bonds only, in the Foundation’s name, and we charge no fees. We never act as a surety, guarantor, or sponsor for any individual. Our fund is small and opens fully after our 501(c)(3) is approved. We can't promise every bond, but we can promise a real person will read this and answer.

What we need to know

  • The full legal name and inmate or booking number of the person held. We verify both with the facility.
  • The jail, the county, and the charge or charges.
  • The bond amount, if you know it. We focus on bonds of a few hundred dollars.
  • A phone number or email where we can reach you.

We help with non-violent charges only. Please don't send Social Security numbers or any ID numbers other than the inmate number. Everything you send stays private with Surrogate Funding Foundation and is never sold or shared.

If you'd rather talk, call or text 803-361-1303.
