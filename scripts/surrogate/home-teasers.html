The founder's story

Wounded in the house of a friend.

After Zechariah 13:6

I came up in the projects. At 21, I built a house from the ground up. I had a mentor I prayed with and broke bread with. Then someone lied on me, and he believed it without ever asking me.

I started Surrogate Funding so young men learn what I learned from someone who has been there, not from a courtroom or a cell.

Lewis BroomeFounder
