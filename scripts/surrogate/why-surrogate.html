The name

Why “Surrogate.”

The vision for Surrogate Funding began with my father, Lewis Broome Jr.

Surrogate means one who stands in another’s place. It comes from the Latin surrogare, “to put in place of another.”

That is the whole work. A mentor stands in where a father, a brother, or an uncle is missing. He does not replace anyone. He shows up. And the fund stands in for the few hundred dollars a family does not have, so a small bond does not cost a man his job, his home, or his children.

Surrogate Funding: standing in, with what it takes.
