Reach him before the system does.

Prevention first · Faith, family, finances, order

I’m a father who was paying what I owed, came up $1,400 short, and watched it become $4,900 plus fees. In that cell, young men called me ‘OG’ and asked me how to live. They didn’t need a lecture. They needed someone to reach them before the system did.

So we mentor first, teach the money lessons I learned the hard way, and keep families together when a small bond would tear them apart.

Lewis Broome IIIFounder & President
