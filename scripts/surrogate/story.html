The founder's story

Wounded in the house of a friend.

After Zechariah 13:6

I came up in the projects. At 21, I built a house from the ground up in a neighborhood where my neighbors were retired military men. I thought I was doing everything right. I had a mentor, a man I prayed with and broke bread with. Then someone lied on me, and he believed it without ever asking me. I had to prove my own innocence before he would even call me back.

That is when I learned what Zechariah meant by being wounded in the house of a friend. Nobody taught me how to see betrayal coming, how to choose my circle, or how to protect what I was building. I learned it the hard way.

I started Surrogate Funding so young men learn it from someone who has been there, not from a courtroom or a cell. Faith, family, finances, and order. Loud and right.

Lewis BroomeFounder
