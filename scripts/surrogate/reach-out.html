For young men

You don't have to carry this alone.

If you've been feeling low, angry, lost, or like nobody is in your corner, tell us. A man who has been where you are will reach back. No judgment, no cost, no one gets in trouble for asking.

If you need help right now

988Call or text the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7, free 911If you or someone else is in danger right now
This page is not a crisis line. We are a small group of volunteers and it can take us a day or two to answer. If you are thinking about hurting yourself, please call or text 988 now.

What happens when you reach out

  • A man from Surrogate Funding reads what you wrote. Only our team sees it.
  • We reach you the way you asked, usually within a day or two.
  • If you want, we connect you with a mentor who meets you where you are: church, a gym, a kitchen table.
  • If you're under 18, we'll ask to bring a parent or guardian into it. We never meet a young man alone.

You can use a nickname. Don't send Social Security numbers or passwords. What you write stays private with Surrogate Funding Foundation, except if we believe you or someone else is in danger, when we'll do what it takes to keep you safe.
