Our mission.

Prevention comes first. Surrogate Funding mentors young men early, before the system reaches them, so they see the trap before they step in it. We teach the money lessons that keep small debts from becoming jail time, and when a small bond would tear a family apart, we post it.

Our vision. Every young man in the Midlands has a man in his corner who tells him the truth before the streets, the wrong friends, or the system do.
