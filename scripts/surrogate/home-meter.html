The pressure

Seven in ten people in jail have not been convicted.

They are waiting. Some are waiting only because they cannot pay a bond of $70 to $300. While they wait, the job goes, the rent goes, and sometimes the children go too.

We take the pressure off at both ends: mentoring young men before the system ever meets them, and bringing people home when a few hundred dollars is all that stands in the way.
